Key Findings & Safety Reasoning

    Architecture

    How LinkShield Evaluates Links

    Every submitted link undergoes multi-layer heuristic scanning to detect malicious intent before you become a victim.

    Heuristic Keyword Signals

    Flags deceptive terminology such as urgent verification, fake banking alerts, lottery cashback, and credential login requests.

    SSL & Protocol Integrity

    Identifies insecure HTTP connections and bare IP addresses that legitimate organizations never use for customer interactions.

    Abused TLD Reputation

    Monitors top-level extensions (such as .xyz, .top, .info) frequently registered by automated scammers due to low barrier pricing.

    Known Threat Intelligence

    Cross-references persistent indicator databases for confirmed fraud campaigns, KYC clones, and reported phishing targets.

    Awareness

    How to Spot a Deceptive Link

    Attackers rely on urgency and clever domain mimicry. Keep these red flags in mind when reviewing unverified messages.

    Impersonation

    Look-alike Domain Names

    Scammers insert hyphens or subtle misspellings (e.g. secure-bank-login.com instead of bank.com) to trick busy users.

    False Urgency

    Urgent Account Actions

    Phrases like "Account suspended in 2 hours" or "Immediate KYC required" are engineered to make you click without verifying the URL.

    Unencrypted HTTP

    Missing HTTPS Security

    Any portal requesting passwords, credit cards, or identity proofs over unencrypted http:// should be treated as dangerous.
